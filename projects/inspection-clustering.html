Inspection Clustering & Route Optimization

Geographic clustering and route-optimization work — with a custom-built HTML interface — that groups field inspections by location and constraints, cutting travel time and improving field-team efficiency.

The Problem

Inspections scattered without regard to geography

Field inspections were spread across a wide area with no systematic grouping, so inspectors often covered inefficient routes and travel time ate into productive hours.

The Approach

Density-based clustering, then route optimization

I used DBSCAN to group inspections into geographic clusters based on density rather than a fixed number of regions, so the clustering adapts naturally to where inspections are actually concentrated. Clustered groups then feed into route/assignment logic so each inspector's day covers a compact, efficient area.
